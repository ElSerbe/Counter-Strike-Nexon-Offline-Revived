Room Settings

Game rules

The game's own options for this mode.

Bots

How the AI players behave in this room.

Skill of new botsThe skill of the AI players you add (AI Players page, Fill room, Quick Start).
Bots that follow ordersWho obeys your radio, chat and C-menu orders: every bot of your team, or only the ones nearest to you. Zombie Shelter's job orders always reach every bot.
Bot messagesRadio messages as text, the game's voices, or silent bots.

Zombie modes

Options for the zombie modes.

Weapon balanceWeapon damage against zombies is evened out by weapon type: strong weapons do less, weak ones more.
GroupsZombie and Zombie Hero modes: human bots hold several spots in groups. Off: they all hold the same spot.
Lone walkersZombie and Zombie Hero modes: a few human bots walk the map alone.
Watch their backHuman bots turn to the nearest zombie they see, even behind them.
Shift + Extra Settings opens the game's own window, with the mode's other options.
